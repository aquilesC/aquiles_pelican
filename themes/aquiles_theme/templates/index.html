Deep-Tech Product & Engineering Leader

I lead the 14-person Product Development team at Orange Quantum Systems (OrangeQS) in Delft, where our engineers and physicists build, deploy, and operate automated testing and characterization systems for quantum processing units (QPUs).

Over the past decade, my work has spanned the full lifecycle of technically complex hardware and instrumentation: managing product roadmaps and semiconductor business development at VSParticle, co-founding Dispertech through pre-seed and seed funding, authoring Python for the Lab, and earning a PhD in Physics at Leiden University.

I write here to organize lessons on deep-tech product strategy, leadership, and science commercialization, complemented by working notes in my digital garden {{ icon('external', 'w-3.5 h-3.5 inline') }}.
