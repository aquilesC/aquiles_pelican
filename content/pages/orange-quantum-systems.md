# Accelerating the Quantum Revolution

Quantum computing is transitioning from pure scientific research into an industrial technology. However, building reliable quantum processors requires testing and characterizing hundreds or thousands of qubits—a process that has historically been manual, slow, and dependent on highly specialized PhD researchers running bespoke scripts.

Orange Quantum Systems builds comprehensive, automated test and measurement systems that radically reduce testing times, enabling quantum chip manufacturers to accelerate their design-fabricate-test-analyze cycles.
