## The Bottleneck in Scaling Quantum Processors

Quantum computing is moving from laboratory demonstrations toward industrial chip manufacturing. Every new generation of quantum processing units (QPUs) requires screening, testing, and characterizing dozens to hundreds of qubits. Historically, that work depended on manual tune-up routines run by specialist PhDs, making testing one of the tightest bottlenecks in the design-fabricate-test cycle.

At [Orange Quantum Systems (OrangeQS)](https://orangeqs.com) in Delft, we build, deploy, and operate automated testing and characterization systems for quantum processors so chip teams can evaluate devices faster and more reliably.

## Leading the Product Development Team

As **Team Lead, Product Development**, I manage 14 direct reports across four core disciplines: software engineering, instrumentation, quantum testing, and systems engineering.

In my first six months, my priorities centered on execution clarity and system integration:

- **Clear Ownership & Faster Learning Cycles:** Defined responsibilities across the unit so engineers and physicists can ship and validate changes rapidly while maintaining minimal system downtime on active testbeds.
- **Ending Software-Hardware Silos:** Shifted how the team plans and builds together, treating cryogenic hardware, control electronics, and automated measurement software as one integrated system.
- **Coaching & Organizational Integration:** Coaching technical leads and individual contributors while building structured interfaces between Product Development, R&D, and commercial teams so market signals and enabling technologies feed directly into our roadmap.

## What Makes Quantum Product Development Distinct

Leading product development in quantum instrumentation requires holding two realities at once. The underlying physics and qubit architectures are still moving fast, yet customers expect dependable systems that operate predictably day after day.

My role is to keep engineering disciplined without losing the scientific curiosity that makes breakthroughs possible: maintaining clear interfaces, tight feedback loops with users, and a cohesive team culture capable of delivering next-generation automated QPU test infrastructure.
