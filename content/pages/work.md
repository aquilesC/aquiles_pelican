## Bringing Deep-Tech Hardware & Software from Lab to Market

Over the past decade, I have worked across the full lifecycle of technically complex instruments: from designing optical experiments and writing acquisition software during my PhD in Leiden, to co-founding Dispertech, owning product roadmaps and semiconductor business development at VSParticle, and now leading the 14-person Product Development team at Orange Quantum Systems in Delft.

My focus sits where engineering execution meets commercial reality: aligning multidisciplinary R&D teams around real customer signals, building reliable hardware-software systems, and turning ambitious research into products organizations can deploy and operate at scale.
